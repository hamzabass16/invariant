# Premium Playbook, v2 (fictional)

A written set of rules for selling options on a small book of large US names.
Everything below is a rule I hold myself to. The account is at one broker.

## 1. Objective

1.1 Sell puts and calls 30 to 45 days from expiry on large, liquid US names
and collect premium. The target is about 2% of the account in premium a
month, so around 20% of the account a year.

1.2 The shares I hold (SPY, AAPL, NVDA) are collateral for covered calls and
a long-term core. I do not trade them.

## 2. Entry

2.1 Only sell premium when the underlying's implied volatility rank is at
least 30. Prefer entries at an IV rank of 50 or more.

2.2 I want to see, for every underlying I have a contract on, today's implied
volatility and its IV rank, every day, on the dashboard.

2.3 For every short option, show the expected move of the underlying to
expiry, so I can see whether the strike sits outside one standard deviation.
Tell me when a short strike is inside the expected move.

## 3. Sizing

3.1 Every short put is cash-secured in spirit: the cash I would need if every
short put were assigned may not exceed 1.5 times the cash in the account.
This is a hard limit.

3.2 The delta-adjusted value of all open options together may not exceed 60%
of the account. Hard limit.

3.3 No more than 25% of the account's premium in a month from one underlying.
This is a guideline I check by hand.

## 4. Management

4.1 Roll or close every position at 21 days to expiry. Tell me as soon as any
open position has 21 days or fewer left.

4.2 Take profit at 50% of the premium collected. Tell me when a short position
has captured 50% or more of its opening premium.

4.3 Calls are only ever sold against shares I hold. I check this myself when I
place the trade.

## 5. Risk

5.1 Tell me when the account is 8% or more below its high.

5.2 The account's beta to SPY over the last year should stay at or under 1.2.

5.3 Tell me when the day's change is a loss of 2% of the account or worse.

5.4 I look at the one-month return, the growth of 100 since inception, the
three-month volatility and the beta on the dashboard, in that order after the
headline.

## 6. Dashboard

Headline, in order: account value, premium collected in the last month,
premium yield over the last twelve months, cash needed if assigned against
cash held, open options' delta value as a share of the account, and positions
with 21 days or fewer to expiry. Then the options panel, then the implied
volatility monitors, then premium by month and profit captured by position,
then risk.
