# Family Investment Policy (fictional)

Adopted by the family council. Applies to everything the office oversees:
bank accounts, bonds and deposits, private funds, properties and the
brokerage account. Figures are in euro.

## 1. Allocation

1.1 Public equities between 30% and 45% of net worth.

1.2 Private funds between 20% and 35% of net worth.

1.3 Fixed income (bonds and term deposits) between 10% and 20%.

1.4 Real estate at most 20% of net worth, measured at our share, net of
mortgages.

1.5 Cash at least 5%.

## 2. Liquidity

2.1 At least 40% of net worth must be liquid: outside private funds and real
estate.

2.2 Cash and deposits must cover every euro the funds can still call, with
room: at least 1.2 times unfunded commitments.

## 3. Concentration

3.1 No single company above 10% of net worth, counting what we reach through
funds as well as what we hold directly.

3.2 No single fund manager above 15% of net worth.

3.3 No single bond issuer above 7% of net worth, government bonds excepted.
We check the exception by hand.

3.4 Exposure to AI and semiconductors, through funds as well, is reported
every quarter and should stay under 25%.

## 4. Fixed income

4.1 Investment grade only. The office checks ratings by hand when a bond is
bought.

4.2 The maturity ladder and what pays back in the next twelve months are
shown on the dashboard.

## 5. Real estate

5.1 Loan to value at most 50% on each property.

5.2 Every property is revalued at least once a year. Tell us when a valuation
is over 365 days old.

## 6. Reporting

6.1 No figure behind net worth may be older than 120 days without the council
being told.

6.2 The dashboard shows distributions and capital calls over the last twelve
months, and each fund's DPI and TVPI as the fund reports them.

6.3 The dashboard shows what we own through the funds, and where a company is
reached both directly and through a fund.

## 7. Governance

7.1 A new fund manager needs a vote of the council. The office records the
vote; the app cannot check it.
