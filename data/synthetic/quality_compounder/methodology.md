# Owner's Manual, third edition (fictional)

How I pick, size and keep businesses. One brokerage account. Every holding is
tagged with its archetype in the app, and my own valuation of each is on
record.

## 1. Archetypes and caps

1.1 Every holding is one of three archetypes, tagged by me: Compounder,
Transitional or Growth. A holding that has failed one threshold is tagged
Probation.

1.2 Position caps, as a share of the portfolio: Compounder 10%, Transitional
20%, Growth 5%, Probation 5%. A cap is a hard limit.

1.3 The three largest holdings together may not exceed 45% of the portfolio.

1.4 Cash is at least 5% of the portfolio.

## 2. Thresholds

2.1 A Compounder has revenue growth of at least 10%, a free cash flow margin
of at least 20%, return on invested capital of at least 20%, and net debt to
EBITDA at most 2x.

2.2 A Transitional business has revenue growth of at least 15% and a gross
margin of at least 50%.

2.3 A Growth business has revenue growth of at least 25%.

2.4 Free cash flow per share must grow for every holding. A fall is a kill
signal.

## 3. Valuation

3.1 I buy only at a price at or below 80% of my own base valuation. Tell me
when any holding trades at or below that level.

3.2 Tell me when any holding trades above 120% of my own base valuation.

3.3 My valuation of each holding is shown next to its price, and must be
under a year old.

## 4. Probation

4.1 A holding on probation is sold if still failing after four quarters: 365
days from the date it went on probation. Tell me at 300 days.

4.2 No new money into a holding on probation. I check this myself.

## 5. Results

5.1 Returns are context, never the verdict. The dashboard shows the one-year
return of the portfolio next to SPY's and QQQ's, growth of 100 since inception
against SPY, the worst drawdown and the beta to SPY.

5.2 The day's change is shown, in money and as a share.

## 6. Dashboard

Headline: portfolio value, cash share, the largest holding's share, the three
largest together, the number of holdings on probation, and the day's change.
Then archetypes and caps, then valuation, then thresholds, then results.
