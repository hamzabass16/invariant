# Three-fund plan (fictional)

One broker, three index funds, a fixed split, a monthly contribution. The
plan is written down so that I do not tinker.

## 1. The split

1.1 Target allocation: 70% world equity (VWCE), 10% world small cap (IUSN),
20% global bonds (AGGH). Measured as a share of the whole account, cash
included.

1.2 Each fund may drift 5 points either side of its target before I
rebalance. Tell me when any fund is outside its band.

1.3 Cash is for the next purchase only: at most 5% of the account.

## 2. Contributions

2.1 I add 1,500 euro on the first of every month and buy whichever fund is
furthest under target. Contributions over the last twelve months should total
18,000 euro.

## 3. Costs

3.1 Fees paid to the broker over the last twelve months must stay under 0.1%
of the account.

## 4. Behaviour

4.1 I do not own single stocks. Any single company position is a breach.

4.2 At most four sales a year. A sale is a failure of the plan unless it is a
rebalance.

4.3 I do not look at returns more than once a quarter. The dashboard shows
the growth of 100 since the start and the worst drawdown since the start, for
perspective, after everything else.

## 5. Dashboard

Headline: account value, the share of each of the three funds, cash share,
and contributions over the last twelve months. Then allocation, then costs
and behaviour, then performance last.
