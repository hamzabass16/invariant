# Product

## Who

Anyone with money in more than one place: an individual with a couple of
brokerage accounts, or a family office with banks, private funds and
property. The owner may not be technical: every step they take is written for
a phone, in plain words, with nothing to install, and a helper can be named to
take the one step that needs a computer.

## What

The same app for everyone, built for each person from their own answers:
their profile and inputs decide the screens, and their methodology decides the
dashboard, its headline figures and the checks. It answers where the money is,
what overlaps, what it is exposed to, and where it stands against the owner's
own written rules, never someone else's.

## Rules

- Read-only. Nothing trades or writes to a bank or broker. Each person's data
  lives in their own Drive.
- Every figure says where it came from and how old it is. Estimates are marked
  as estimates, and a document that cannot be read never looks empty.
- A drafted methodology changes nothing until its owner confirms it.
- Not investment advice.
